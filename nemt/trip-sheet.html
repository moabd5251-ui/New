Read this before trusting the output. Defaults are 2026 national mid-ranges assembled from published rate and insurance surveys — they are a starting point for your own arithmetic, not a quote. Rates, licensing, and premiums vary enormously by state. Replace every number with one you got from a phone call.

What this does not model: self-employment and income tax, the 30–90 day payment lag on broker trips (a cash-flow problem separate from profitability), insurance down payment at binding, licensing and startup costs, unpaid time spent scheduling and billing, or a vehicle replacement reserve.
