inline without research. That matters: a state licensing inbox will answer six numbered
questions and ignore three paragraphs of narrative.

## Sending notes

- **Send them separately**, not as one email to both addresses. Different teams; a combined
  email tends to get half-answered.
- **Send Tuesday through Thursday, mid-morning.** Monday and Friday inboxes at state agencies
  are the worst.
- **If no reply in 5 business days, call.** TSE is 360-902-3703. Phone often beats email at
  licensing desks, and you can ask follow-ups in real time.
- **Save the replies.** Written answers from the licensing authority are worth keeping — if a
  rule is ambiguous later, an email from DOL saying how they read it is the best
  documentation you can have.
- **Don't overstate your stage.** You're researching, not applying. That's true, it's easier
  to answer, and it keeps the conversation open.
