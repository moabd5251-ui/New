# 4. Branding prompts (ChatGPT, or any image model)

Reconstructed from the video's 17:16–19:48 section. Nothing here is
ChatGPT-specific — any image model does it.

## 4a. Channel name

```
Give me 30 channel names for a YouTube Shorts channel about [interesting facts
about NICHE].

Rules:
- One word, or two short words.
- Pronounceable and spellable after hearing it once.
- No numbers, no hyphens, no deliberate misspellings, no "TV"/"HQ"/"Daily"
  suffixes.
- Avoid anything that reads as a real company or an existing large channel.

Group them: 10 concrete nouns, 10 abstract/evocative, 10 invented words.
```

The video's advice not to overthink this is correct. Pick in under five
minutes.

**Then check it's free** before committing: search the name on YouTube, and
check the handle is available at `youtube.com/@yourname`. The video skips this
and it's the one part of naming that can actually cost you a re-brand.

## 4b. Logo

```
Create a YouTube channel logo for a channel called "[NAME]" about [NICHE].

- Square, 1:1, at least 800x800.
- One single central symbol. No text, no letters, no wordmark.
- Must stay readable at 48x48 pixels — that is the size viewers actually see it
  at in the Shorts feed. Thick shapes, high contrast, no thin lines, no fine
  detail.
- Flat vector style. Maximum 3 colours plus the background.
- Dark background, one bright accent colour.
- Centred with even margin, nothing touching the edges.
```

The 48px note is the whole game. Logos are designed at 800px and seen at 48px,
which is why detailed ones turn to mush in the feed. The video's own examples
support this — a panda, a slouching figure, a book. One shape each.

## 4c. Banner

```
Create a YouTube channel banner using the same colour palette and design style
as the logo above.

- 2560x1440 pixels.
- ALL text and key artwork must sit inside the centre 1546x423 region — that's
  the only part visible on mobile. Everything outside it is background only.
- Centred text, large and bold: "[SHORT LINE]"
- One simple icon beside the text: [ICON].
- Flat, minimal, generous empty space. No photos, no gradients, no drop
  shadows, no stock-template look.
```

The 1546×423 safe area is the piece the video doesn't mention, and it's why
AI-generated banners so often end up with the text cropped off on phones — the
model centres the text in a 2560×1440 canvas, and YouTube crops the sides away.

If the text comes out small, the video's fix works: `make the text about 50%
bigger, keep everything else identical`.

## 4d. Channel description

```
Write a YouTube channel description for "[NAME]", which posts short
interesting-facts videos about [NICHE].

Format exactly:
- Line 1: a tagline under 10 words. This is the only line most people see.
- Blank line.
- 2-3 sentences: what the channel covers and what a viewer gets from it.
- Blank line.
- One line: posting frequency.
- Blank line.
- One line: a call to subscribe.

Plain text. No emoji, no hashtags, no keyword stuffing, no "welcome to my
channel", under 150 words. Write it in a flat, factual voice — no hype
adjectives.
```

## 4e. Thumbnail — not needed

Shorts don't use custom thumbnails in the feed; YouTube pulls a frame. Don't
spend time here. The first frame of your video *is* the thumbnail, which is an
argument for making segment 1 visually strongest.
